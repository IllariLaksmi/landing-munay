Munay means *unconditional love* in Quechua. It is also the name of a promise: that healing should not depend on where you were born, how much you earn or how much you are willing to explain yourself.

## The gap we could not ignore

For years, in classrooms, community halls and small rooms across Peru and beyond, we met people who had no safe place to talk about their bodies. Survivors of abuse, assault, trafficking, torture and intimate partner violence were often sent from one waiting list to another. Sexual education, when it existed at all, arrived wrapped in shame. And the few holistic services available rarely spoke the language of trauma, consent or culture.

We founded Munay in 2026 to answer that gap.

## What we do

Munay offers workshops, individual services and one-day retreats for underserved communities. Our work weaves positive sexual education with somatic and holistic healing rooted in Andean wisdom. Every space is designed to be accessible, community-led and informed by the realities of the people we serve.

- **Workshops** on sexuality, consent, boundaries and body literacy.
- **Individual services** for mind, body and soul, from intuitive therapy to Reiki and pelvic floor care.
- **Day retreats** that bring rest, movement and community into the same room.

## What comes next

Our first community gathering will be a Zumbathon. The date is still being confirmed, but the intention is already clear: joy is part of healing, too. Subscribe to our newsletter and we will let you know as soon as the date is published.

If you are a survivor, an educator, a healer or a neighbor who believes care should reach everyone: welcome. Munay is built in reciprocity, and there is room for you here.
